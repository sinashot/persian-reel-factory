# Viral playbook (what worked on real Persian Instagram pages)

## Topic
- A **broad, everyday worry** people will send to family: "the pill in every home", "the mistake
  everyone makes". Narrow topics get views but no shares; shares are what push a reel out.
- Numbered lists (۳ تا ۵ مورد) beat essays. Name concrete, familiar things (a brand, a habit, a tool).
- A series ("قسمت اول") turns viewers into followers: the CTA promises part 2.

## Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen from frame 1)
Pick one formula and make the first spoken line and the hook title say it:
- **Warning:** «این ۵ تا رو هیچ‌وقت با هم ...» / «اگه ... می‌کنی، داری ... رو دور می‌ریزی»
- **Hidden in plain sight:** «شماره‌ی ۳ توی خونه‌ی شماست»
- **Myth:** «همه فکر می‌کنن ...، ولی ...»
- **Number + payoff:** «با یه کار ساده، ... نصف شد»
- **Proof in the video itself:** «همین ویدیویی که می‌بینی رو ... ساخته»
Add a small badge that makes people stay: «شماره‌ی ۳ رو حتماً ببین».

## Body
- One chapter per item, each starts with its number spoken («یک، ...»). 6–9 s per chapter.
- Each chapter: name (title) → what it is (visual) → why it matters (chip on the key word).
- One emotional peak in the middle (danger tint, flash or a counter hitting its number).

## CTA (one ask, last 6–8 s)
Choose one, say it and show it:
- Follow for part 2 (series) — best for followers.
- Comment a keyword («کلمه‌ی «ریل» رو کامنت کن تا لینک رو بفرستم») — best for reach + DMs.
- Send to someone («بفرستش برای کسی که ...») — best for shares.
End on the same idea as the hook.

## Caption
- Line 1 (before "... more"): the hook rephrased + the payoff, under ~110 characters.
- 3–6 short lines of value or the list, then the same single ask as the video.
- 3 hashtags at most, specific (not #explore). Add search words in plain Persian in the text.
- Sensitive topics (medicine, money, law): a one-line disclaimer, and a professional checks the
  script before posting.
